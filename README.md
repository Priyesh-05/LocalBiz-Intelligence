# LocalBiz Intelligence

An intelligent business analytics platform designed to help small and local businesses understand their performance, identify opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

## Status

🚧 Early Development

## Vision

LocalBiz Intelligence aims to transform raw business data into actionable insights that help business owners understand:

- What is generating revenue and profit
- Where money is being wasted
- What products or services attract customers
- How to improve customer retention
- Whether expanding to a new location makes business sense
- How to respond to periods of poor performance

## Planned Features

- Business performance analytics
- Product/service profitability analysis
- Customer behavior and retention analysis
- Revenue and cost analysis
- Business trend detection
- Local market intelligence
- AI-powered business insights
- Decision-support recommendations
